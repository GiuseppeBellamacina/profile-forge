## Secret trophies

66 of them. They are only drawn when earned; `ranks: ["SECRET"]` shows just these and `hideRanks: ["SECRET"]` hides them all. Each has a **Title** you can pass to `titles` / `hideTitles`.

<img src="docs/guide/trophies-secret-3.png">
